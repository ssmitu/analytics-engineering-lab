# Last Statement Analysis

## Objectives
- Identify recurring themes in last statements
- Detect religious references, forgiveness, prayer, and innocence claims

## SQL Techniques Used
- Conditional filtering (`WHERE`, `LIKE`)

## Findings
- **Religious references**: Many inmates mention God directly.
- **Forgiveness**: Several statements include appeals for forgiveness, either spiritual or directed at victims’ families.
- **Prayer**: Some inmates explicitly mention praying or ask others to pray.
- **Claims of innocence**: A subset of inmates use their final words to assert innocence or deny guilt.

These themes highlight both spiritual expression and contested narratives at the end of life.
